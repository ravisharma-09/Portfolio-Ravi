Ravi Sharma

Developer and Computer Science student at Newton School of Technology

I'm Ravi Sharma, a B.E. Computer Science student at Newton School of Technology (2025–2029). I build working products: LessonLingo, an offline lecture translator for Hindi and Kannada; Setu, a sign-language bridge built for Smart India Hackathon 2026; and Pathglass, a graph query engine written from scratch.

In open source I've had fixes merged into the openSUSE Open Build Service and Uyuni, and I'm part of the openSUSE community (openSUSE.Asia Summit 2026). In competitive programming I placed All India Rank 17 in Turing Cup 2k26 and competed at ICPC Asia Amritapuri 2025.
