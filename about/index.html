Setu: Sign Language Bridge (Smart India Hackathon 2026)

Team project · Sep 2026 · TypeScript, React, Python, MediaPipe

Fixed camera recognition of face-adjacent signs by capturing full body, hand and 48-point face trajectories; bundled MediaPipe Holistic for offline use with GPU-to-CPU fallback; rebuilt sign playback as a 2D signer with a resumable dataset pipeline (4,384 clips).
